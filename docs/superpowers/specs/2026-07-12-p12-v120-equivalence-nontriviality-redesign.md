# P12 v1.2.0 Equivalence and MR Non-Triviality Redesign

**Status:** Approved design awaiting written-spec review  
**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Target:** P12 independent evidence infrastructure consumed by P3 for a TOSEM-level confirmatory study

## 1. Objective

P12 v1.2.0 shall deliver a common, auditable infrastructure for:

1. inducing abstract semantic mutation operators from an independent 12-project D1 development corpus;
2. instantiating the frozen operators on the 20-project confirmatory frame to generate controllable semantic mutants;
3. screening equivalent mutants without using MR outcomes;
4. retaining the existing 20-project D2 corpus of real historical buggy/fixed revisions;
5. comparing native LLM MR recognition (B0) with structured recognition using meta-patterns and MR Families (B1);
6. applying a shared, arm-blind operational MR non-triviality theory before portfolio construction; and
7. evaluating B0 and B1 on the same frozen controlled-mutant and real-fault denominators.

The design is outcome-neutral. It tests whether and where B1 improves on B0; it does not presuppose a favorable result.

## 2. Governance and Versioning

This is a scientific-design change, not a transport correction. It changes the role of D1, the mutant denominator, the MR arms, and the confirmatory interpretation. Therefore:

- the new producer/consumer contract version is `v1.2.0`;
- P12 W3.4/v1.1.2 and its hashes remain immutable historical evidence;
- a governed amendment shall record `scientific_impact=true` and `changes_confirmatory_interpretation=true`;
- v1.2.0 shall create a new P3/P12 cross-repository content-hash anchor;
- the existing D2 commitment shall remain unopened;
- no current Task 18-23 or W4 execution may be represented as v1.2.0 evidence;
- v1.2.0 requires a new workflow freeze and a new W4_PREOPEN gate.

## 3. Evidence Partitions

### 3.1 D1-12 operator-development partition

The existing 12-project, 176-family D1 corpus is used only to induce, merge, split, audit, and freeze the abstract `CORE_OPERATOR_SET`. D1 shall not contain D2 identities, patches, outcomes, or derived templates.

Each core operator shall define:

- `operator_id` and semantic-fault class;
- applicability preconditions;
- source-pattern and transformation rule;
- controllable parameters;
- expected semantic effect;
- trigger and observability requirements;
- known equivalence risks;
- generator implementation/version/hash;
- supporting D1 evidence identifiers.

### 3.2 Twenty-project target partition

The 20 projects shared by D2 and the MR tracks are the confirmatory project frame. Frozen core operators may be instantiated on these projects, but the target projects may not change the confirmatory abstract operator taxonomy.

New abstract operators discovered while examining the 20 projects shall be recorded in `TRANSFER_EXTENSION_SET` and used only for exploratory analyses. Project-specific applicability sites and operator parameters are not new abstract operators and may be added before target-mutant sealing.

### 3.3 D2 real-fault partition

D2 retains its 94 real historical semantic-fault families from 20 projects. Buggy and fixed revisions originate from upstream developer history; P12 may author reproduction harnesses and oracles but may not fabricate defects or fixes.

Before MR outcomes are available, an independent role shall map each D2 fault to a semantic-fault class and, when justified, to the closest frozen semantic operator. Unmappable faults remain in D2 as `UNMAPPED_REAL_FAULT` and may not be removed.

### 3.4 MR partitions

- **B0 LLM-native:** candidate MRs generated without meta-pattern names, MR Family lists, structured recognition rules, D1 operators, D2 faults, or any detection outcome.
- **B1 structured:** candidate MRs generated by the explicit chain `public interface -> meta-pattern -> MR Family -> project API binding -> source/follow-up transformation -> relation oracle`.

B0 and B1 use the same project material, LLM/model versions, seeds, token budgets, candidate caps, repair rounds, execution-confirmation budgets, and final portfolio size. The recognition method is the intended treatment difference.

The existing 83-MR corpus may enter B0 only if its immutable generation prompts and context prove that meta-pattern and MR Family information was absent at generation time. Otherwise it is retained as calibration evidence and B0 is regenerated.

## 4. Mutant Equivalence Protocol

### 4.1 Domain-relative definition

For original program `P` and mutant `Pm`, equivalence is defined only relative to a frozen tuple `(alpha, X, E, tau)`:

- `alpha`: committed semantic-output abstraction;
- `X`: committed input domain/distribution and finite boundary set;
- `E`: source, dependency, toolchain, and runtime environment locks;
- `tau`: output distance, numeric tolerance, terminal-state, exception, crash, and timeout semantics.

The project protocol must be frozen before target mutant generation.

### 4.2 Asymmetric evidence rule

One stable semantic-divergence witness is sufficient to establish non-equivalence. Finite output agreement is never sufficient to establish equivalence.

A non-equivalence certificate records the input, source/mutant revisions, raw output hashes, semantic projections, distance/tolerance result, repeated-run evidence, environment hash, and terminal status.

Only the following may establish `CERTIFIED_EQUIVALENT`:

- normalized AST or executable identity under the frozen build;
- exhaustive enumeration of a finite committed domain;
- independently checkable SMT/symbolic equivalence proof;
- independently checkable dead/unreachable-code proof over the committed domain;
- independently checkable overwrite-before-read data-flow proof; or
- independently checkable algebraic-identity proof under the committed preconditions.

LLM or human opinion, an all-zero MR kill row, test-suite survival, lack of reachability under current tests, or finite sampled agreement cannot independently certify equivalence.

### 4.3 Mutant states

Every generated mutant receives exactly one latest append-only state:

| State | Primary denominator treatment |
|---|---|
| `INVALID_MUTANT` | excluded; reported as generation failure |
| `DUPLICATE_MUTANT` | deduplicated and excluded; original link retained |
| `CERTIFIED_EQUIVALENT` | excluded with certificate |
| `CONFIRMED_NON_EQUIVALENT` | included |
| `OPERATIONALLY_INDISTINGUISHABLE` | included |
| `UNRESOLVED_EQUIVALENCE` | included |
| `INFRA_UNRESOLVED` | reported separately; never relabelled as a scientific result |

`NOT_REACHED` and `NO_INFECTION` are diagnostic observations, not equivalence verdicts. They cannot remove a mutant from the primary denominator.

### 4.4 Frozen denominator and sensitivity bounds

The primary conservative denominator is:

`N_primary = N_viable - N_certified_equivalent`.

The primary lower-bound mutation score is:

`MS_lower = N_killed / N_primary`.

`OPERATIONALLY_INDISTINGUISHABLE` and `UNRESOLVED_EQUIVALENCE` remain in `N_primary`. A witness-only sensitivity upper bound is reported as:

`MS_upper = N_killed / N_confirmed_non_equivalent`.

If `N_confirmed_non_equivalent` is zero, `MS_upper` is reported as undefined rather than imputed or replaced.

The denominator and all pre-execution equivalence states are sealed before B0/B1 execution. A later MR kill may append a non-equivalence witness but may not alter the frozen confirmatory denominator or erase earlier uncertainty.

## 5. Operational MR Non-Triviality Theory

P3 adopts the operational subset of the four-layer theory from the method seed. The full undecidability proof, all strict-separation witnesses, vacuity transfer, and broader theory remain outside P3's main contribution until independently completed and verified.

### 5.1 L0 validity

G0 checks the candidate against the correct reference/specification model using bounded property testing, boundary cases, deterministic seeds, and frozen tolerances. A counterexample yields `REJECT_SPEC_INVALID`. Absence of a bounded counterexample yields `BOUNDED_VALIDITY_SUPPORTED`, not a universal proof. Timeout or unavailable oracle yields abstention.

### 5.2 L1 semantic grounding

G1 verifies that the relation oracle factors through the committed semantic abstraction `alpha`. Access to incidental logs, formatting, timestamps, memory layout, or uncommitted output fields yields a rejection certificate. Unresolved grounding yields abstention.

### 5.3 L2 non-triviality witness

G2 searches only independent D1-12 calibration mutants, toy/spec models, bounded SMT models, or other preregistered non-confirmatory assets for a program/input that violates the MR. A witness yields `CERTIFIED_NONTRIVIAL_WITNESS`. Exhausted bounded search yields `ABSTAIN_NO_WITNESS`, not an intrinsic-triviality claim.

### 5.4 L3 pool-relative characterization

The target-mutant and D2 kill rows are unavailable to G0-G2. After confirmatory execution, an all-zero row is recorded as `POOL_ZERO@pool_hash`; a nonzero row is `POOL_NONTRIVIAL@pool_hash`.

L3 is an outcome and may not be used to regenerate, replace, delete, or re-admit B0/B1 MRs. Zero rows remain in all recognition-yield and end-to-end effectiveness denominators. They may be removed only in a separately identified post-outcome minimal-subset analysis over the already committed matrix.

### 5.5 Arm blinding

The G0-G2 evaluator receives opaque batch and candidate identifiers and may not see B0/B1 arm identity, D2 identities, target-mutant outcomes, hypotheses, or anticipated result direction.

P12 v1.2.0 shall add versioned schemas and append-only ledgers for:

- semantic mutation operators;
- operator evidence and taxonomy decisions;
- project-specific operator applicability;
- mutant instances and patches;
- project semantic-observable contracts;
- mutant equivalence assessments and certificate bundles;
- native/structured MR generation provenance;

- MR validity/grounding/non-triviality certificates;
- D2 semantic-class/operator mappings;
- frozen mutant denominator;
- B0/B1 portfolios;
- controlled-mutant and D2 execution rows;
- post-outcome pool-relative MR characterization.

All scientific identities, decisions, failures, abstentions, and supersession links are append-only and content-addressed.

## 7. Analysis Populations and Estimands

The study reports the complete B0/B1 funnel:

1. generated candidates;
2. schema-valid and executable candidates;
3. L0 bounded-validity-supported candidates;
4. L1 semantically grounded candidates;
5. L2 witness-certified and L2-abstained candidates;
6. frozen portfolio members;
7. target-mutant nonzero/zero rows;
8. D2 real-fault nonzero/zero rows.

Two estimands are mandatory:

- **End-to-end recognition effectiveness:** candidates that obtain at least one confirmatory detection divided by the fixed generation budget/candidate count and reported cost.
- **Conditional admitted-MR effectiveness:** detection performance among MRs passing the frozen pre-outcome gate.

Primary outcomes include conservative mutation score, operator-macro detection, real-fault RFDS, unique detections, B0/B1 complementarity, and cost-normalized effectiveness. Results are reported per project, operator, semantic-fault class, and MR Family, with project/fault hierarchical inference and the frozen two-level BCa procedure.

## 8. Hard Invariants and Negative Controls

The implementation shall fail closed on at least the following attacks:

- D2 patch, mechanism, or outcome entering operator induction or MR generation;
- target-project evidence altering `CORE_OPERATOR_SET`;
- meta-pattern/MR Family text entering B0 generation context;
- B0/B1 unequal model, token, repair, execution, or portfolio budgets;
- arm identity visible to G0-G2;
- all-survive or zero-row evidence certifying mutant equivalence;
- finite sampled equality certifying equivalence;
- `NOT_REACHED` or `NO_INFECTION` removing a mutant;
- unresolved mutants silently removed from the primary denominator;
- target-mutant or D2 kill rows used by G2;
- L3 zero rows removed from end-to-end results;
- post-execution amendment of the mutant denominator;
- unmapped D2 faults removed;
- failed, rejected, abstained, timeout, crash, or infrastructure cases deleted;
- v1.1.2 artifacts overwritten or represented as v1.2.0 results.

## 9. Stage Gates

The repaired workflow uses these sequential gates:

1. `V120_AMENDMENT_ACCEPTED`
2. `EQUIVALENCE_PROTOCOL_FROZEN`
3. `CORE_OPERATORS_FROZEN`
4. `TARGET_MUTANTS_GENERATED`
5. `MUTANT_DENOMINATOR_SEALED`
6. `B0_B1_MR_SETS_SEALED`
7. `D2_FAULT_MAPPING_SEALED`
8. `W4_PREOPEN_V120_PASS`
9. separately authorized one-time D2 opening
10. `W4_FINAL_V120_PASS`

Failure of a gate stops progression. A blocked gate is recorded with negative evidence; policy, schema, denominator, or sample identities may not be weakened after outcomes to obtain a pass.

## 10. Acceptance Criteria

The design is ready for implementation planning when the written specification is accepted with all of the following fixed:

- v1.2.0 versioning and preservation of v1.1.2;
- certificate-only exclusion as the primary mutant-equivalence policy;
- conservative primary denominator and explicit sensitivity bound;
- D1-12-only induction of core operators;
- 20-project operator instantiation without confirmatory taxonomy expansion;
- auditable B0 contamination check or regeneration;
- paired and budget-equal B0/B1 protocol;
- arm-blind G0-G2 MR gate;
- G2 restricted to D1/non-confirmatory assets;
- G3/L3 moved to post-outcome analysis;
- D2 retained unopened until the new W4_PREOPEN and P3 statistical hard gates pass.
