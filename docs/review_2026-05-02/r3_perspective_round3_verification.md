## 3. Residual non-blocking nits

### 3.1 W6 ISO 26262-8 §11 TCL 2/3 language not invoked verbatim

The §6.5.1 air-gap declaration at line 1493 names ISO 26262 by family-spec number, but does not invoke the *tool qualification* clause specifically (ISO 26262-8 §11.4.6 "the tool shall be deterministic, or non-determinism shall be detected and handled"). §7.1.7 R10 (lines 1596-1603) describes Claude Opus subscription's lack of seed control and lists three mitigations (multi-turn de-dup, K=10/20 repetitions, raw response store), but does not declare *"this non-determinism makes SMS computation incompatible with ISO 26262-8 TCL 2/3 tool qualification."* The W4 air-gap declaration plus the §7.1.7 R10 mitigation set is, on net, sufficient for an IST V&V-engineering reader to draw the right conclusion themselves; an additional verbatim ISO 26262-8 sentence would harden the claim. **Non-blocking** for Round-3 verdict — the *substance* is now correctly disclosed across §6.5.1 + §7.1.7. A future minor edit could add one sentence like *"In ISO 26262-8 §11 terminology, this non-determinism profile would require detection-and-handling provisions before any TCL 2/3 qualification claim, which is out of P2's research scope."*

### 3.2 §6.5.2 line 1499 "scientific computing domains" parenthetical

§6.5.2 line 1499 retains unqualified phrasing in *"MR designers (often researchers or senior developers in scientific computing domains)"*. Under the line 1480 §6.5 Scope cabinet, this is a *stakeholder-population description* (who the readers are) rather than a *methodological-applicability claim* (where the method is applicable), and is therefore not a structural problem. The §1.1 rewrite explicitly authorizes this usage as a "general domain term." **Non-blocking**, but a one-word tightening to "scientific computing kernel domains" or "computational science domains" would reduce reader-facing ambiguity at zero cost.

### 3.3 §6.5 broad-domain occurrences elsewhere

I re-counted occurrences of "scientific computing" in §6.5 (lines 1478-1536): line 1480 has the qualified phrase "single-output scientific computing kernels" inside the Scope paragraph; line 1499 (3.2 above); line 1524 *"V&V documentation for scientific computing software"* — this is *describing the existing-V&V-practice domain*, not a P2-scope claim, and is correctly contextualized by the surrounding "scoped to V&V documentation, not normative certification" hedge. Net: scope-discipline within §6.5 is now adequate. The §1.1 line 38 rewrite plus the §6.5 Scope leading paragraph operate as the two structural mechanisms; the body text's three remaining unqualified mentions are reasonable usages under those mechanisms.
