## Verdict (accept/minor/major/reject)

**Major revision.** The core idea (an MR-adequacy denominator over admitted domain-semantic mutants, backward-compatible with MS) addresses a real gap, and the failure reporting (H1–H4 all missed) is unusually honest. But the formal layer is oversold relative to its actual content, the operator taxonomy contradicts itself in the paper's own examples, one headline empirical claim ("structurally unreachable") overstates the evidence, one result (30/30 real-defect face) is circular by inclusion rule, and foundational MT/mutation prior work is missing. All are fixable without new experiments except possibly the AST-overlap reanalysis.

## Blockers

1. **[§"Degeneration to Classical Mutation Score" + supp. Appendix G] Theorem 1 is a definitional substitution presented as a limit theorem.** Lemma G.3's "proof" is "L5 switches mut_j to syntactic operators… under this configuration mut_j(S_i) is the literature-defined syntactic mutant set" — assuming the conclusion — and the "almost everywhere w.r.t. D_S" qualifier is ill-typed because SMS is a ratio of finite cardinalities per cell, not a function of inputs; restate as an exact identity under stated substitutions with a separate measure-zero caveat on the E2 classifier, and stop counting it as one of three "proofs" answering RQ1.
2. **[supp. Appendix G.3, Lemma G.1] The lemma proves the wrong E1.** The proof dispatches E1 as "type consistency (trivial)," but the paper (§"Equivalence Judgement") defines E1 as AVP coherence over MR_{i,k}; the degeneration of the AVP-coherence conjunct under L is never actually established.
3. **[§"Effect map, fibers, and three theorems", Theorem 2] The undecidability theorem is folklore claimed as contribution.** It is Rice plus a halting reduction, its classical analogue (equivalent-mutant undecidability, Budd & Angluin 1982) is uncited, and RQ1(a) presents it as a novel theorem of the paper.
4. **[§"Effect map, fibers…", σ definition + S5] The effect map is not well-defined and stratum purity is never verified.** σ assigns each edit a unique class, but nothing guarantees a mutant violates only one ψ; S5 is required "where stratum labels feed downstream" — they do (the aligned-vs-cross analysis is the headline result) — yet the empirical pipeline never checks S5, and the off-diagonal kill mass is prima facie evidence of S5 violations, undermining the fiber-partition reading of j=k vs j≠k.
5. **[§"Decoupling between Semantic Feasibility and Kill Rate" vs Table 2 (operator signatures)] The five operator families leak into each other in the paper's own examples.** c3_HP1 is "relu → tanh" (an Operator Substitution by the paper's definitions) and c3_TF1 is "max_iter 1000 → 5" (a Hyperparameter per the HP class-a spec "tolerance / max_iter"), so family boundaries are inconsistent exactly where the aligned-vs-cross construct needs them crisp.
6. **[Table tab:p2-05, Fig. 3 caption, Highlights] "Structurally/categorically unreachable" is overstated for HP.** HP mutants are AST-local single-literal edits (the paper's own necessary-conditions table marks HP "×" on condition (a)), so the 0/72 overlap reflects cosmic-ray's default value menu, not structural unreachability; only SI and TF plausibly warrant the structural claim, and the exact-AST-match metric compounds the bias (a near-miss NumberReplacer variant of an HP mutant counts as disjoint).
7. **[§"RQ4: Industrial Construct-Separation Evidence"] The 30/30 real-defect face is circular.** Inclusion required "an MR-detectable behavioral difference under the declared stratum," so pattern-derived detection of every admitted case is guaranteed by the admission rule; the safeguard given ("no case dropped after observing comparative results") does not cure this, and the text must state that the 30/30 face is selection-conditioned, with evidential weight residing only in the baselines' blindness on the same cases.
8. **[§Related Work + throughout] Foundational MT/mutation prior work is missing.** No Chen et al. 1998/2018 (MT origin and CSUR survey), no Segura et al. 2016 TSE survey, no Liu, Kuo, Towey & Chen TSE 2014 — the canonical precedent for assessing MT effectiveness via mutation, i.e., the direct methodological ancestor of SMS — no Kanewala & Bieman scientific-software-testing line, no Budd & Angluin 1982; for a TOSEM MT/mutation audience these absences are disqualifying as submitted.

## Major concerns

- **Duality theorem is a tautology doing conceptual, not mathematical, work.** Theorem 3 says an MR whose violation predicate factors through α is constant on α-equivalence classes — one line from definitions, with "MR validity" (r does not flag P) smuggled in as an unstated hypothesis that the strong/weak definition later shows to be contingent. It is fine as a framing lemma; it is not one of "three theorems" warranting the RQ1 claim "answered by construction and proof." The ε_tol-governed boundary (Prop. 2) and its empirical map (§RQ4 strong-boundary) are genuinely the most valuable formal-empirical content — the sensitivity–specificity coupling is correct and well-instantiated by the PINN and numpy-RNG cases — and the paper would be stronger if it led with that instead of the theorem count.
- **Homonym risk with Clark-style SMT is not disambiguated.** Clark/Dan/Hierons semantic mutation testing mutates *language/semantic-variation-point interpretations* (what the developer might have misunderstood about the semantics), which is a different construct from domain-invariant-certified code edits; the related-work paragraph cites them correctly but never states the difference, so the paper's title term collides with a 15-year-old, differently-defined technique. One explicit contrast paragraph is needed.
- **On question (c): the definitional distinction is coherent, the operationalization is not yet.** "Intensionally semantic, extensionally syntactic" plus S1–S5 is a real, specification-relative distinction — this is *not* merely HOM with a label, because membership is defined by invariant violation, not edit arity. But its cash-value depends on verifying S3/S5, which the pipeline approximates by bounded sampling and (for S5) not at all; and the "certificates" are test-evidence records, not proof objects (admitted in Limitation 9). Either rename ("evidence record") or flag the non-proof-carrying nature at first use of "certificate," since RQ1 currently derives "certificate-based" from the undecidability theorem as if certificates discharged what testing cannot.
- **The HOM rebuttal is rhetorical.** "Exponentially unlikely… on combinatorial grounds" (§Higher-Order Mutation Boundary) is asserted without any calculation over the actual operator menus and PUT ASTs; either supply the combinatorial estimate or delete the probabilistic language and keep only the honest "confined to first-order defaults, HOM not refuted."
- **Alignment map align(j)=j is declared a design choice, then treated as the discriminant.** With the family leakage (Blocker 5), the b-class inversion, and no justification for pairings like OS↔monotonicity, the diagonal's privileged status needs a sensitivity check (e.g., permuting the alignment map and showing the observed diagonal beats permuted alignments); otherwise the operator-to-meta-pattern alignment reads as ad hoc inheritance from the P1 infrastructure.
- **Empirical support is thin on its own terms and the Conclusion oversells it.** All four pre-registered hypotheses fail, 45/60 cells are zero, mean suspect_share is 0.791, and 3/12 PUTs give zero signal everywhere; the reframing as "boundary findings" is legitimate, but "the empirical study supports the semantic-mutation construct in seven ways" (§Conclusion) should be tempered to diagnosability claims.
- **Weak-MR false positives cannot occur inside SMS.** killed(s′, MR) requires AVP(S_i, mr) = pass, so a weak MR produces no kills at all; Prop. 2(i)'s false-positive mode manifests as MR-validation failure upstream, not SMS inflation — reconcile the boundary narrative with the killed predicate explicitly.
- **Fabrication-adjacent literature claim.** "§RQ3: LLMorpheus reports high cross-MP failure proportions on JavaScript scientific-computing PUTs (specific numbers not listed in cited literature)" — LLMorpheus has no MP/MR construct at all; citing a paper for a finding it does not report, with a parenthetical admitting the numbers don't exist, must be deleted or replaced with a verifiable claim.
