## Verdict (accept/minor/major/reject)

**Major revision.** The paper's honesty infrastructure is unusually strong (verdicts reported as "not met," an inference-permissions table, declared confounds), but the submission contains unverifiable pre-registration claims, several load-bearing numbers with conflicting or missing provenance, an arithmetically suspect equivalence-bias argument, and at least two places where the narrative exceeds its own licensed readings. None of these is fatal to the construct; all of them block publication in the current text.

## Blockers

1. **[Throughout; §Hypotheses, §Statistical pipeline]** The term "pre-registered" carries the entire H1–H4 argument and the "boundary delimitation" reframing, yet no registration artifact (registry entry, DOI, timestamp, or frozen protocol document) is cited anywhere, making the claim unverifiable; the industrial arm's "pre-registered in the dataset protocol" is self-referential to the authors' own Zenodo deposit.
2. **[§4 intro / §8 Conclusion]** With all four pre-registered hypotheses failing, the paper never states what empirical outcome *would* have counted against the SMS construct, so the "failed thresholds map construct boundaries" reframing is unfalsifiable as written; a disconfirmation criterion (pre-specified or explicitly labelled post hoc) must be added for the reframing to be methodologically legitimate rather than rationalization.
3. **[§RQ3 distribution vs §RQ4 aligned-vs-cross]** Two different 95% bootstrap CIs are given for what reads as the same headline delta ([0.127, 0.740] in the effective-n note vs [0.014, 0.622] v4 / [0.017, 0.622] v3), with no statement of which estimand or bootstrap each belongs to.
4. **[§Root-cause results (tab:p2-13) vs §Cross-source discussion]** The identical numeric pair 0.164 → 0.209 is attributed once to LRCA threshold calibration (default vs ood_band = 0.02) and once to v3→v4 cross-source pooling ("+27%"), so the repeatedly used claim that "cross-source improves kill-set quality" may be a calibration artifact; the two axes must be disentangled (and supplementary §A.2 reports 0.200, not 0.209, for the calibrated best).
5. **[§Industrial arm + supplementary Appendix I]** The arm verifies 34 cases but the detection face reports 30/30 with no accounting of the 4 excluded cases, and the "four-group" comparison never enumerates its groups (Appendix I describes what appears to be five tracks: semantic, two generic, two ablated), so the arm's headline numbers are not traceable to a defined design.
6. **[Abstract]** "Aggregate kill-rate, semantic alignment, and real-defect detection can rank the same relations in *opposite orders*" exceeds both the reported evidence (the pattern-derived group ranks first on both measured faces; only the ablation kill-rate/detection divergence is shown) and the licensed readings in Table tab:inference-permissions; this is a silent upgrade at the paper's most visible location.
7. **[§Equivalence judgement, §Prescreen, supplementary A.3]** The claimed bias direction of E1∧E2 is arithmetically wrong or unexplained: misclassifying truly equivalent mutants as non-equivalent adds unkillable survivors to the denominator and biases SMS *low*, yet the text says it "biases SMS slightly high" while calling this "conservative," and the A.3 trade-off table is internally inconsistent (E1-alone shrinks the denominator yet is labelled "Low" bias).
8. **[§Equivalence judgement / Limitation 1]** The "Hoeffding-style upper bound on false-equivalence probability" said to be given in the equivalence section appears nowhere in the main text or supplement, and the key tolerances ε_eq and the per-MP ε_AVP^k values are never numerically specified, so neither E2 nor the kill oracle is reproducible from the manuscript.
9. **[§Prescreen/AVP]** The AVP version is "pinned" to a literal unfilled placeholder, `<AVP-vX.Y>`, in the submitted manuscript.
10. **[§MR coverage matrix + §RQ4]** The 6 vacant cells (no MR exercised) are declared "not formally adjudicated" yet are counted inside the 48-cell cross slice and the 60-cell H2/H4 statistics; including structurally-zero cells mechanically inflates the aligned-vs-cross delta and the 0.275-vs-0.061 separation, and a sensitivity analysis excluding them is required.
11. **[§LRCA diagnosis + supplementary A.2/C.4]** The L0 artefact pre-scan and the C5 "artefact recheck" both presuppose the dual-blind reviewer machinery that the primary v4 pool skips, yet the H4 verdict (suspect_share 0.791) is rendered on v4; the paper must state how L0 and C5 attribution were actually executed for unreviewed v4 mutants.

## Major concerns

- **H2 statement mixes registration with hindsight.** The "pre-registered" H2 in §Hypotheses embeds post-hoc qualifications ("the design is underpowered at this boundary, so its verdict is read as a point-estimate result"), and the power analysis that justifies this reading is built from the observed data (plug-in bootstrap, mixture calibrated to the observed zero-mass). The demarcation between what was registered and what was learned afterwards must be explicit, or the paper is HARKing-adjacent even while being honest about verdicts.
- **Protocol asymmetry: well disclosed, poorly quantified, and broader than acknowledged.** Disclosure at points of use (§Cross-source protocol, §RQ4, §Threats, §Protocol-asymmetry estimate) is commendable. But (i) the ±0.03–0.05 δ-shift bound rests on an unsourced "5–10% filtered" figure and unshown arithmetic; (ii) the asymmetry is discussed only for the source-axis contrast, while H1, H3, and H4 verdicts are also rendered on the v4 pool, meaning the measurement instrument changed relative to the (presumably v3-protocol) registration; this second exposure is never analyzed.
- **The withdrawn data-driven primary-MP shift is untraceable.** §Primary MP convention reports a permutation-null analysis that motivated withdrawing an exploratory primary-MP reselection, but no numbers, procedure, or appendix location are given; the reader cannot audit the very analysis that guards against selection-on-the-response.
- **Industrial-arm circularity is stronger than acknowledged.** Cases are admitted only after MR-detectability verification under the declared stratum; if the registering relation is the pattern-derived one, 30/30 detection is close to tautological, and the informative content is the baselines' blindness. The text's "face-level check" caveat is good but should state the circularity mechanism explicitly, and the Wilcoxon/Holm analysis needs its family size and group definitions (δ = +0.247 with Holm-adjusted p = 0.046 is fragile).
- **E1∧E2 soundness on stochastic classes is unspecified.** For MCMC, MC, and NN-training PUTs, E2's per-sample comparison ‖S(x) − s′(x)‖ and the AVP kill oracle require seed control or distributional comparison; nothing is said, and OR-aggregation across MRs whose verification is an α = 0.05 statistical test inflates false kills — consistent with suspect_share 0.791, which implies most cross-slice "kills" are non-semantic. The SMS_C1 sensitivity partially mitigates this, but the primary metric's meaning in cross cells needs franker treatment.
- **Effective-n arithmetic is internally inconsistent.** §RQ3 says ~18 observations "carry nonzero SMS (12 aligned + ≈6 nonzero cross)" while the same section reports 3/12 aligned cells zero and §Root-cause reports 15/60 nonzero cells; 9 + 6 = 15, not 18.
- **Main/supplement desynchronization.** The supplement (dated one day earlier) contradicts the main text in several verdict-relevant places: "All 37 operators produced ≥ 1 V1-V6 passing mutant (verifying H1)" vs H1 not met; V1-V6 vs V1-V4 gate counts; "cell-level claims use BH-FDR" (§D.1) vs main's "no cell-level correction is claimed"; C1_share 0.200 vs 0.209; 12 vs 13 cosmic-ray default operators (§B.2 vs main §HOM). These must be reconciled in the submitted package.
