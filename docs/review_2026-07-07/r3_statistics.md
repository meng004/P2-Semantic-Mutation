## Verdict (accept/minor/major/reject)
**Major revision.** The statistical honesty machinery (inference-permissions table, stipulated-alternative power, explicit degeneracy handling) is well above the norm and mostly self-consistent, but there is at least one outright numerical inconsistency, the industrial arm's headline inferential result is under-reported to the point of irreproducibility from the manuscript, and the abstract reads two statistics above their licensed rows.

## Blockers

1. **[§rq1-60-cell-distribution] Inconsistent headline CI.** The effective-n paragraph cites "the wide 95% bootstrap CI [0.127, 0.740] (upper/lower ratio ≈ 5.83)" for the aligned-vs-cross delta, but §rq2-aligned-vs-cross-cliffs-delta reports v3 CI [0.017, 0.622] and v4 CI [0.014, 0.622]; the [0.127, 0.740] interval matches neither and appears to be a stale value from an earlier pool version, so one of these numbers is wrong or unlabeled.
2. **[§rq4-industrial-construct-separation] The Holm result is not reproducible from the manuscript.** The only inferential claim on the industrial arm ("mean paired difference +0.101, Holm-adjusted p=0.046, Cliff's δ=+0.247") is reported without the test's direction (one-sided is nowhere stated in the main text), the family definition (the 3 comparisons under Holm are never enumerated), the four group identities, the unadjusted p-values, or a CI on the paired difference.
3. **[§rq4-industrial-construct-separation] 34 vs 30 is never reconciled.** The arm is introduced as a 34-case paired study but the detection face is 30/30; the disposition of the other 4 cases is unexplained anywhere in the body, which is exactly the gap a selective-reporting reading exploits.
4. **[§rq4-industrial-construct-separation] The 30/30 face is circular by construction and this is not stated.** Admission requires "an MR-detectable behavioral difference under the declared stratum" with each case "register[ing] the metamorphic relation that discriminates the two"; if the registered discriminating relation is (or overlaps) the pattern-derived relation, 30/30 detection is guaranteed by the inclusion rule, so the face evidences only the baselines' blindness, and the manuscript must say which relation family did the admission screening.
5. **[§rq4-industrial-construct-separation] No fragility analysis for a marginal p=0.046.** A Holm-adjusted p this close to 0.05, on an effect that shrank when the pool grew from 30 to 34 cases, needs a leave-one-out / sign-flip sensitivity note and an explicit statement that n=34 was fixed before the comparison was run; none is present.
6. **[Abstract] Two statistics are read above their inference-permission rows.** "…rank the same relations in opposite orders, confirming the paper's construct separation on real defects" (i) asserts a rank reversal that is never explicitly exhibited in the main text (the body shows divergence and non-nesting, not an order inversion between named relations), and (ii) uses the 30/30 face, licensed only as a "face-level count," as confirmatory evidence, violating the paper's own rule that "no number in this paper should be read above its row."

## Major concerns

1. **Stipulated power of 49.1% is criterion-intrinsic, not a sample-size finding (item e).** For the decision rule "point estimate ≥ threshold," power at truth = threshold is ≈50% for any approximately median-unbiased estimator at any n; presenting 49.1% as evidence the "design is underpowered at this boundary" (H2 statement, permissions table, Conclusion item 3) attributes to the (12, 48) design what is a property of the pre-registered decision rule. The text partially concedes this ("increasing the sample size … cannot lift the point estimate") but the framing should be corrected. Additionally, given the documented discontinuity (any ε>0 jumps δ from 0.314 to 0.74), the mixture alternative (w=0.094, +0.001 shift) is one of infinitely many alternatives with E[δ]=0.4746, and the power figure is alternative-shape dependent; a sentence acknowledging this non-uniqueness is needed.
2. **Odds-ratio criterion was ill-defined at pre-registration and is a misnomer (item b).** A ratio of group medians of a continuous score is not an odds ratio; and zero-inflation making median(cross)=0 was foreseeable, so the criterion could only be infinite or undefined in half the plausible outcome space. The handling (declare trivial satisfaction, zero evidential weight, fail H2 on delta) is honest and logically sound since H2 is conjunctive, but a well-defined binarized sensitivity (e.g., odds of nonzero SMS: aligned 9/12 vs cross 6/48 gives OR ≈ 21) should be reported, clearly labeled non-pre-registered.
3. **The source-axis contrast CI is invoked but never reported.** Δδ = −0.009 "(95% CI covers zero)" is used three times (§rq2-aligned-vs-cross, §cross-source-contributes, Conclusion item 4) without the interval's endpoints ever appearing; a CI used to support a scoped null must be printed.
4. **Friedman labeling is internally inconsistent (item a/d).** The permissions table licenses it as "exploratory inferential," §rq3-cross-class calls it "the non-parametric formal alternative" and flags p=0.0041 "(significant)," the caveat paragraph in the same subsection calls the per-class Friedman "descriptive only" while reporting Bonferroni-adjusted p-values, and Conclusion item 5 reports χ²=15.30, p=0.0041 with no exploratory qualifier. Pick one label per statistic and use it everywhere.
5. **"Confirming" on descriptive class means.** §h4-partial-across-same-source-and-cross-source: "confirming that c/d classes have higher mutant-diversity demand than a/b" draws a dispositional conclusion from two uncorrected descriptive percentage changes (+91.5%, +38.3%) that have no row in the permissions table; soften to "consistent with."
6. **Holm family membership is ambiguous for the ablation claim (item d).** "The pattern-derived group does not significantly dominate its ablations under the same denominator" is a significance statement; whether these ablation contrasts are inside the 3-comparison Holm family or an uncorrected side family is indeterminable from the text. All other families are clean: the cell-level non-family is explicitly disclaimed, per-class Friedman carries Bonferroni×4, and H1/H3/H4 are count-threshold verdicts carrying no p-values.
