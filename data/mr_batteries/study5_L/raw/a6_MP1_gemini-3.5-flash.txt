The program solves $r^3 + r = 4x - 2$. Under the transformation $x \to 1 - x$, the right-hand side becomes $4(1-x) - 2 = -(4x - 2)$, which negates the target value, resulting in the negated root: $r(1-x) = -r(x)$. 

To formulate this as a strict **conservation** relation (where the output property remains completely invariant under the input transformation), we can define transformations that conserve either the absolute value of the output, or conserve the output itself by applying a double reflection (which trivially acts as the identity, but we can also use properties of the symmetry to construct non-trivial invariants).
